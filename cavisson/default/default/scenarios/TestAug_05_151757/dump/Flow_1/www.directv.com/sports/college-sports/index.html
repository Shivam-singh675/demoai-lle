Frequently asked questions

  • What channels can I watch college sports on?

    While some college sports are available to watch on channels like ESPN, ABC, NBC, CBS and other national networks, there are also multiple college-specific sports channels available, including ESPNU, ACC Network, SEC Network, Big 10 Network and more. You can find all those channels in a variety of DIRECTV TV plans.

  • What is the best TV plan for someone looking to watch live college sports?

    DIRECTV has the channels you need to watch college sports live. You can get national networks, college specific channels and more with the complete DIRECTV Signature CHOICE TV Plan or through MySports Genre Pack, a curated TV plan with a channel lineup of just sports, including ESPN Unlimited on us!
