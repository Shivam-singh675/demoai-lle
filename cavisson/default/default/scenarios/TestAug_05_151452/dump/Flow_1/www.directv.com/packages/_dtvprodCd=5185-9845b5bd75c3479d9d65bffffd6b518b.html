FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

  • What are the available Signature DIRECTV packages and prices?

    • ENTERTAINMENT ($69.98/mo. with $10/mo. Gemini lease fee. Price reflects $30 off for 1st mo.; $5 for mos. 2-24.) 90+ Channels
    • CHOICE ($89.98/mo. w/Regional Sports Fee up to $19.99/mo. & $10/mo. Gemini lease fee. Price reflects $30 off for 1st mo.; $10 for mos. 2-24.) 125+ Channels
    • ULTIMATE ($99.98/mo. w/Regional Sports Fee up to $19.99/mo. & $10/mo. Gemini lease fee. Price reflects $35 off for 1st mo.; $15 for mos. 2-24.) 160+ Channels

  • What is the DIRECTV Gemini device?

    • DIRECTV provides you the ability to watch TV over your existing home internet connection with our exclusive device called Gemini™. Gemini allows you to access thousands of streaming apps (Netflix, HBO Max, Prime Video and more) -- all from within the DIRECTV experience – no need to switch inputs. Gemini’s voice remote lets you discover shows, change channels and more without lifting a finger – just ask Google.Learn more.
    • Google login required. High speed internet required. Separate subscription/login required for 3rd party app content.

  • Do I need a Satellite dish to get a DIRECTV package?

    No, DIRECTV customers can choose to sign up for one of the following DIRECTV plans:
    • DIRECTV Satellite +Device
    • DIRECTV Streaming App + Device
    • DIRECTV Streaming App Only via Internet (like Hulu+ Live TV & YouTube TV)

  • Does DIRECTV have a “basic” TV package?

    • DIRECTV ENTERTAINMENT is our entry level package, which includes all your essential channels packed with entertainment, news, and sports —TV everyone will love. 

  • Are local channels included in DIRECTV plans?

    • Yes, local channels like ABC, CBS, FOX, NBC, PBS, and The CW are included in every package at no additional cost, where available.

  • Does DIRECTV have TV packages in other languages?

    • DIRECTV offers Spanish packages along with 20+ International TV channel add-ons in many languages: Spanish, Brazilian, Cantonese, Mandarin, Filipino, Japanese, Italian, Korean, Russian, and Vietnamese.

  • What types of DIRECTV packages are there?

    DIRECTV has a variety of TV packages to fit a wide range of customer preferences:
    • DIRECTV's Signature Packages are complete live TV packages with 150+ channels
    • Genre Packs are specifically curated channel lineups that allow you to get the genre of channels you want, without paying for all the ones you don't.
    • MyFree DIRECTV is a completely free way to watch live TV & On Demand content. Find the MyFree channel lineup here.

  • How can I record my TV shows, sports, and movies?
